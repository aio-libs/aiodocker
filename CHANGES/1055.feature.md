Added image reference parsing helpers, ported from Docker's [reference](https://github.com/distribution/reference) implementation and exported from the top-level package: `split_docker_domain()` splits a reference into its registry domain and remainder, `split_image_reference()` splits it into name, tag and digest, and `is_valid_domain()` validates a registry domain. The related `DEFAULT_DOMAIN`, `LEGACY_DEFAULT_DOMAIN` and `OFFICIAL_REPO_PREFIX` constants are exported as well.
